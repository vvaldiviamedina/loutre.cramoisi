Grammar

Articles

French nouns have a gender (masculine or feminine) and the article must agree with it.

le chat (m.), la table (f.), les livres (pl.)
the cat, the table, the books
un chat (m.), une table (f.), des livres (pl.)
a cat, a table, some books

Present tense: être (to be)

Basic sentence structure

Standard word order is Subject – Verb – Object, similar to English.

Je mange une pomme.
I eat an apple.

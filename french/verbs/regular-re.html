Regular -re verbs

Most French verbs ending in -re follow this same pattern.

Vendre (to sell)

PronounConjugation
jevends

Other -re verbs

Conjugate these the same way as vendre — swap the stem, keep the endings (-s, -s, -, -ons, -ez, -ent).

FrenchEnglish
AttendreTo wait (for)
EntendreTo hear
DescendreTo go down
RépondreTo answer
PerdreTo lose
RendreTo give back

Not all -re verbs follow this pattern — prendre (to take) and être are common irregular exceptions.

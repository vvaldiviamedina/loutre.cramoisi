Regular -er verbs

Most French verbs ending in -er follow this same pattern.

Other -er verbs

Conjugate these the same way as parler — swap the stem, keep the endings (-e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent).

FrenchEnglish

A few -er verbs have small spelling tweaks to keep their pronunciation consistent, e.g. manger → nous mangeons, acheter → j'achète.

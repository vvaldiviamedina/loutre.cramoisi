Regular -ir verbs

Most French verbs ending in -ir follow this same pattern.

Other -ir verbs

Conjugate these the same way as finir — swap the stem, keep the endings (-is, -is, -it, -issons, -issez, -issent).

FrenchEnglish
ChoisirTo choose
RéussirTo succeed
GrandirTo grow (up)
RemplirTo fill
ObéirTo obey
AgirTo act
RougirTo blush
RéfléchirTo think / to reflect

Not all -ir verbs follow this pattern — some, like partir, sortir, and venir, are irregular.
